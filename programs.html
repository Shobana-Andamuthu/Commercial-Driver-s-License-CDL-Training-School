FMCSA Aligned Curriculum

CDL Training Programs & Endorsements

Every training track at Apex combines Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) classroom theory, dedicated private yard backing practice, and actual highway driving hours.

Tractor-Trailer Certification

CDL Class A Training Course

The gold standard for commercial driving careers. Qualifies you to drive 53-foot tractor-trailers, dry vans, reefers, and flatbed combinations.

WEEK 01 40 Hours

ELDT Permit & Air Brakes

Classroom & computer lab instruction covering federal regulations, electronic logbooks, vehicle dynamics, and CLP permit exam clearance.

Milestone: State CLP Permit Issued
WEEK 02 40 Hours

110-Point Pre-Trip Walkaround

Hands-on component diagnostics: brake chambers, air leakage tests, coupling mechanics, steering linkages, and fluid checks.

Milestone: Pre-Trip Inspection Mastery
WEEK 03 40 Hours

Closed-Course Range Backing

Mastering straight-line, sight-side and blind-side offset backing, 90-degree alley dock maneuvers, and coupling on our 10-acre pad.

Milestone: Yard Skills Sign-Off
WEEK 04 40 Hours

Interstate Highway & DMV Test

Public road shifting, highway merging, urban intersection clearance, followed by the official DMV test administered on our private pad.

Milestone: Official Class A CDL License
Heavy Straight Vehicle

CDL Class B Training Course

Prepare for lucrative local and regional careers driving commercial straight trucks, dump trucks, cement mixers, and utility vehicles.

Course Structure

120-Hour Professional Class B Track

Designed for fast, high-quality certification. Includes 30 hours classroom theory, 45 hours range yard maneuvering, and 45 hours road navigation.

  • Full-time: 3 weeks (Mon-Fri, 7:00 AM - 3:30 PM)
  • Weekend option: 6 weeks (Saturdays & Sundays)
