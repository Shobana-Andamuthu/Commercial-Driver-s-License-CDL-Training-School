About Apex CDL Academy

Established to bridge the nationwide driver shortage with rigorous, safety-first commercial driver education. We produce competent, confident drivers who excel on testing day and in everyday freight logistics.

Core Purpose

Our 4 Core Training Standards

Building life-changing career pathways while keeping continental highways secure through rigorous commercial training standards.

Safety-First Culture

Every movement begins with situational awareness, mirror scanning cadence, and proactive defensive positioning.

Repetition & Muscle Memory

Students perform over 150 live backing reps and 110-point walkarounds so test-day execution is instinctive.
