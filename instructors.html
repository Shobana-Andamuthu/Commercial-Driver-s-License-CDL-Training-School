Pedagogy

Our Behind-The-Wheel Coaching Protocol

A structured three-stage progression that transforms raw beginners into confident, test-ready professional commercial drivers.

PHASE 01 • FOUNDATION

Dual-Control Range Physics

Instructors demonstrate clutch friction points, trailer pivot angles, and mirror alignment while you observe in-cab, followed by hands-on low-speed pad maneuvers.

  • Straight-line and offset backing repetition
  • 110-point verbal walkaround articulation
PHASE 02 • PRECISION

Alley Dock & Parallel Mastery

Calm, guided one-on-one radio coaching as you execute 90-degree alley docks, blind-side offsets, and tight commercial loading bay alignments.

  • Zero-shouting, step-by-step mirror sweeps
  • Unlimited remedial range backing practice
PHASE 03 • HIGHWAY

Live Traffic & Road Evaluation

Advance to real interstate corridors, multi-lane roundabouts, bridge height awareness, and simulated official CDL road test examination routes.

  • Defensive spacing cushion mastery
  • Full mock DMV skills exam with score sheet
