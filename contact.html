Help & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers to the most common questions from prospective commercial truck driving students.

What are the DOT physical and drug screening rules?

Federal law requires all commercial drivers to hold a valid DOT Medical Examiner's Certificate (Form MCSA-5876) and pass a 5-panel drug screen before behind-the-wheel range instruction begins. We assist with local medical appointments.

Do I need my Commercial Learner's Permit (CLP) beforehand?

You do not need your CLP before enrolling. Week 1 of our program includes intensive CLP theory instruction, sample practice tests, and air brake knowledge to ensure you pass your state permit exam on the first attempt.

Is tuition assistance or WIOA grant funding accepted?

Yes. Apex is an approved training provider for Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA) grants, Trade Adjustment Assistance (TAA), and GI Bill® education benefits. Flexible monthly payment plans are also available.

Do you offer weekend or evening training cohorts?

Yes. In addition to our full-time 4-week weekday program, we offer an 8-week weekend track (Saturdays and Sundays from 7:00 AM to 5:30 PM) so you can earn your Class A CDL without leaving your current job.
