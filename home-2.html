Your 4-Stage Roadmap to Licensure

A linear competency pipeline designed to take students from zero commercial vehicle experience to a certified Class A or B CDL holder.

01
Stage 1: Classroom Theory

Commercial Permit (CLP) Prep

40 hours covering ELDT regulations, hours-of-service electronic logging devices (ELD), air brake systems, and combination vehicle physics.

Deliverable: State CLP Permit Issued
02
Stage 2: Yard Inspection

110-Point Pre-Trip Walkaround

Master engine component naming, slack adjusters, brake chambers, kingpin locking mechanism, and the critical in-cab applied air brake leakage test.

Deliverable: Pre-Trip Yard Clearance
03
Stage 3: Range Control

Closed-Course Backing Control

60 hours of spatial awareness training: straight line, 90-degree alley docking, and sight-side/blind-side offset maneuvers between regulation cones.

Deliverable: Yard Maneuvers Signed Off
04
Stage 4: Road & DMV

Interstate Highway Driving & Exam

60 hours of real-world public road driving covering gear downshifting, bridge height clearance, ramp merging, followed by the official DMV test.

Deliverable: Official Commercial Driver's License

Skills Testing Standards

Master The 4 Core Backing Maneuvers

Our private 10-acre paved facility allows students to practice all 4 state-mandated test maneuvers until muscle memory is perfected.

Maneuver 01

Straight Line Backing (100 Feet)

Back a 53-foot combination tractor-trailer straight through a 12-foot wide boundary lane without touching boundaries or excessive pull-ups.

  • Requires precise subtle steering adjustments and dual mirror reference
  • Trained to completion in Week 2 of yard sessions
Maneuver 02

Offset Backing (Sight-Side & Blind-Side)

Navigate the vehicle from one traffic lane into an adjacent parallel lane located to either the left or right, maintaining trailer alignment.

  • Teaches pivotal turning angles and visual reference points
  • Includes get-out-and-look (GOAL) safety protocol practice
Maneuver 03

90-Degree Alley Docking

Back the tractor-trailer into a perpendicular loading dock bay at a sharp 90-degree angle simulating real distribution center facilities.

  • The most challenging maneuver on the official state CDL examination
  • Dedicated one-on-one coaching in 6 private alley dock bays
Maneuver 04

Parallel Parking (Conventional & Sight-Side)

Park a 70-foot overall combination rig alongside a simulated urban curb between front and rear boundary markers within 3 minutes.

  • Simulates city delivery parking and designated rest stop stalls
  • Full tire clearance and rear bumper alignment validation
